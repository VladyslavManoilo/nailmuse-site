Privacy Policy

Your manicure is personal.
Here’s how your information is handled.

1. Who this policy covers

Nail Muse is an iPhone manicure try-on app operated by the independent developer of Nail Muse (“we”, “us”). This policy covers the current app and this support website. For privacy questions or requests, contact vladislavmanoilo@gmail.com.

The app does not require an account. Camera images, selected photos, nail masks and hand-position estimates are processed on your iPhone to display the manicure preview. We do not receive those images or estimates through the app.

2. Camera and selected photos

  • Camera: access is requested when you start live try-on. Frames are processed on the device. Nail Muse does not upload or continuously record a video of your session. Capturing a frame moves a still image into photo mode.
  • Photo picker: the app imports the image you choose through Apple’s photo picker. It does not browse or upload your full photo library. If an original is stored in iCloud, Apple may download it to your device to complete your selection.
  • Detection: the bundled model finds nail areas and may estimate hand position for fitting. These results are used for the visual effect, not to identify a person. They are not sent to us or used by us to train a model.

The current image and detection results are held in memory while the app is in use. Nail Muse does not maintain a persistent in-app photo gallery or a server-side image archive. Temporary operating-system caches are managed by iOS.

3. Preferences, saving and sharing

Your language choice is stored locally in the app’s preferences. The app does not create a remote user profile. iOS may include preferences in device backups according to your Apple settings.

Saving or sharing happens only when you use the system share sheet. If you save an image to Photos or Files, that copy is managed by you and the selected app or service. iCloud Photos or other backup services may sync it according to your settings. If you send an image to someone or another app, the recipient or service handles that copy under its own practices.

Nail Muse does not automatically send your manicure to a nail artist or to the developer.

4. Analytics, advertising and accounts

The current app has no account system, advertising SDK, third-party analytics SDK or developer-operated crash-reporting service. We do not use your camera images or selected photos for advertising, profiling or model training, and we do not sell them.

Apple may process App Store or device diagnostic information under your Apple settings and its own privacy practices. This is separate from the app’s local image processing. See Apple’s privacy information.

5. When you contact support

If you email vladislavmanoilo@gmail.com, we receive your email address, message and any attachments or device details you choose to include. We use them to respond, investigate the issue and handle any follow-up. Photos are optional; please share only what is necessary and avoid unrelated personal information.

Support mail is handled through Gmail. Google and the email services involved in delivery process those messages under their applicable privacy practices; see Google’s Privacy Policy. Email providers may process information in countries other than your own.

We keep correspondence only for as long as it is reasonably needed to resolve the request, provide follow-up or meet applicable legal obligations. You can ask us to delete your support correspondence. We may retain information where required by law or needed to address a legal claim, and email-provider backups may take additional time to expire.

6. This website and external links

This website is designed for GitHub Pages. It has no analytics scripts, advertising, embedded third-party content, contact forms, or cookies set by Nail Muse. Fonts and images are served with the website.

When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. Hosting and associated technical data are governed by the GitHub Privacy Statement. GitHub describes this logging in its Pages documentation.

Following an external link opens a separate service, which may collect information under its own policy. Clicking an email link opens your email app; it does not send a message automatically.

7. Your choices and deletion

  • You can revoke camera access in iPhone Settings. Photo mode remains available without live camera access.
  • To clear an in-memory photo session, fully close the app. Deleting the app removes its locally stored preferences; offloading an app may keep its data.
  • Copies you saved in Photos, Files, messages or cloud services must be deleted there separately, including Recently Deleted folders or backups where applicable.
  • For access, correction or deletion of support information you sent us, email vladislavmanoilo@gmail.com. We may ask for information reasonably necessary to verify the request.

We have no server-side account or image collection to search or delete. These choices do not limit any additional privacy rights available to you under applicable law, including the right to contact your local data-protection authority where applicable.

8. Children’s privacy

Nail Muse is not specifically directed to children and does not ask users to register or submit personal information to try on a manicure. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us through support, contact vladislavmanoilo@gmail.com so we can review and remove it as appropriate.

9. Changes and contact

We will update this page and its effective date if the app’s data practices change. Material changes will be described before the relevant new processing begins, with consent requested where required.

Privacy contact: vladislavmanoilo@gmail.com.
